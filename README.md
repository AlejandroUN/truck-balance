# Balance Mula

Trip and expense tracking for owner-operator truck drivers.

Log every haul in seconds from your phone and always know what each trip actually left you after fuel, tolls and everything else.

## Features

- **Trips:** origin, destination, freight, advance and payments received, with a reminder of what's still owed.
- **Trip costs:** fuel, tolls, parking, loading and unloading, per diem, tire service and more. Type sums like `150000+30000` right into the field.
- **Truck costs:** social security, insurance and tires.
- **Household expenses:** each one is assigned automatically to the latest trip.
- **Monthly and all-time summaries:** freight, costs, truck balance and owner balance, with a month-by-month chart.
- **Built for the road:** installs on Android and iPhone like a native app, opens instantly, and saves entries offline until there's signal again.
- **PIN protected:** no data is stored in this repository.

## Install

Open the app in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone) and choose **Install app** / **Add to Home Screen**.
